**Title:** Simplify Buzzer Connection Management

**Description:**  
- Make UDP broadcast the single source of truth for buzzer connections.  
- Remove mDNS and NVS fallbacks.  
- Implement an ACK mechanism for server messages.

**Urgency:** High  
This change is crucial for improving the reliability and efficiency of the buzzer connection management. 

**Expected Outcome:** Streamlined management of buzzer connections, reducing complexity and increasing performance.
